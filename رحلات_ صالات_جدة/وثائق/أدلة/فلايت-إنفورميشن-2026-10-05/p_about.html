Flightinformation.com

Are you interested in live flight tracking and detailed aviation information? In that case, you have come to the right place. At Flightinformation.com we collect live data from multiple sources and combine it with our own systems to make flight information both accessible and reliable. The website is created by a dedicated four-person team, and the focus is presenting processed, easy-to-understand live flight data.

A new project built on long experience

While Flightinformation.com itself is a new website, at Westcoast Digital we have many years of experience building and running flight-related websites. For example, we run Flightsfrom.com which is the ultimate tool when you are looking for scheduled flights.

With that being said, this is our first full investment in real-time aviation data – a project which we have created with a lot of care.

Built together with our users

The idea behind this project is that it is meant to grow together with its visitors. Everything from features and data to presentation and priorities will be shaped over time based on feedback from the people who actually use the service.

The long-term plan is to gradually integrate Flightinformation.com into our wider network of flight-related services on the web, making live flight data a natural part of everything we build.

Our data sources

  • Wingbits - Live aircraft positions (ADS-B + ADS-C).
  • Cirium - Flight schedules and route data.
  • Kayak - Pricing and market.
  • Amadeus - Airline and airport information.
  • OpenStreetMap contributors & Maptileserver.com - Used for map tile data.
  • FlightsFrom - Airport and route data.

Privacy and cookies

Flightinformation.com respects your privacy. We only process personal data if you actively choose to contact us or subscribe to a newsletter by entering and confirming your email address. If a newsletter is used, your email address may be stored by a third-party provider, such as Mailjet, which may process data outside the EU.
