How billing works
One call, one credit
Pricing is usage-based and predictable: every successful request spends one credit, whichever endpoint you hit. No per-field charges, no surprise multipliers.
Failed requests and cached repeats within a lookup don't cost extra. Your live balance and per-endpoint breakdown are always visible in the console.
Flight monitoring is billed the same way: creating a monitor costs one credit, then one credit for each alert we deliver to your webhook. Alerts cover departures and landings, early or late departure and arrival estimates, gate, terminal and baggage-belt assignments or changes, schedule changes, cancellations and diversions. No alerts, no charge.

Questions
Frequently asked
Do I need a credit card to start? No. Create an account, generate a key and you get 100 call credits immediately, no card and no trial clock.
What counts as a call? Each successful API request spends one credit, regardless of endpoint. Errors don't count, and repeated internal fetches within a single lookup are billed once.
What happens when I run out of calls? The API returns a clear quota message instead of charging you unexpectedly. Upgrade to a paid plan — Starter ($49), Growth ($99) or Scale ($449) — for more monthly calls, or talk to us about Enterprise volume.
Can I upgrade or change plans later? Yes. Start free and move to Starter, Growth or Scale whenever your volume grows. Nothing to re-learn: the endpoints and response shape stay identical across every plan.
Where does the flight data come from? A blend of live position feeds and schedule sources, unified behind one response. We operate our own live network so a single upstream outage doesn't take your integration down.
Are there rate limits? Generous defaults on every plan to keep the service fast for everyone. Growth and Enterprise raise them to match sustained production traffic.
